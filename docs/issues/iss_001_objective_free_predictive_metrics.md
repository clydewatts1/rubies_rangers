## 1. Context & Concept (The Why)
To achieve genuine predictive edge ('alpha') over the public template, Rubies Rangers must rely on leading, forward-looking metrics rather than backward-looking trailing metrics (past points, past goals, or pundit opinions). Goals in football are Poisson-distributed rare events; trailing form creates sample noise and recency bias.

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives
- Identify and formalize zero-cost, objective, forward-looking predictive metrics from open endpoints.
- Formulate mathematical models for Post-Shot xG (xGOT delta), Penalty Area Touches, Box Conceding Vulnerability, and Team xG share.
- Integrate meteorological conditions (wind speed, precipitation, temperature) into pitch dampening factors.
